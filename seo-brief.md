## Financial-value and evidence controls

- The calculator is an editable planning aid. Its default example uses USD 300,000 current technology run cost, USD 250,000 annual manual-work value, and USD 5,000,000 addressable procurement spend. At illustrative assumptions of 10%, 15%, and 1%, it shows USD 117,500 in potential annual gross value.
- The estimate deliberately excludes Oracle subscriptions/licenses, implementation, integrations, data remediation, change management, tax, financing, and productivity ramp. Capacity value is not automatically cash savings.
- Calculator values and assumptions are appended to the lead message so the consultation can start from the prospect’s inputs.
- Guardia-delivered regional projects are separated from Oracle-published global customer results. The Guardia cases are explicitly identified as Oracle database, core-banking, or multi-platform transformations—not Oracle Fusion ERP references.
- Oracle customer outcomes link to Oracle’s own Sandhar, Atlassian, and Avis Budget Group stories and are labeled as illustrative outcomes, not Guardia projects, benchmarks, or guarantees.
