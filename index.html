FAQ

Questions before you begin

Ask an ERP specialist
Where can Oracle ERP reduce operating cost?

From a simpler technology estate, less repetitive finance and procurement work, tighter spend control, and better visibility into cash and projects.

What does an Oracle ERP implementation include?

Typically discovery, process design, configuration, integrations, data migration, testing, training, go-live and support, scoped to your operating model.

Can Oracle ERP integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. Integration is planned during solution design, including legacy and third-party systems.

How long does an ERP implementation take?

It depends on scope, entities, modules, integrations and data quality. A discovery phase sets a realistic timeline.

Can we move from a legacy or on-premise ERP in phases?

Often, yes. Phasing entities, modules and integrations balances continuity, risk and time to value.
